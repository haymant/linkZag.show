## Why declarative?

- **Portable.** The same scene renders in the studio, as a PNG, or as a ten-second video.
- **Reviewable.** Diff the scene, not a forty-slide deck.
- **Deterministic.** Time is an input; there are no hidden timers.

| Surface | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Studio | interactive preview |
| Capture | PNG frame sequence |
| Segment | PNG + MP4 + manifest |

> If the scene is data, the video becomes a build artefact.
